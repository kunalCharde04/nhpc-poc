## Layout (U-shape, counters 0.60 deep, aisle ~1.5 m)
- Hob (3-burner) on the east wall, close to the SE corner; cook faces EAST. Gas cylinder in the SE corner base unit.
- Sink under the west window (W3), 0.7 m offset from the hob so fire and water are not in line.
- Fridge in the SW corner (against the south/west walls).
- Prep counter + microwave between hob and doorway; base units with drawers on the south wall.
- Wall units 0.35 deep over the east and south runs; keep the window wall clear.
- Kitchen balcony (west): optional washing machine / utility.

## Finishes / Vastu colours
Cream / light-yellow laminate base, off-white wall units, tan-brown or beige counter (avoid black), pale yellow or cream wall tiles. Avoid black or dark blue on the east and north.
