# B/H-105 – Living & Dining (Step 1 of 5)

Source: approved plan (Building 'B' type, flat B/H-105; typical floor). Drawing is north-up, entrance D1 at the SW corner of the living room (stair-lobby side).
Room: 3.60 x 5.80 m. West wall: entrance D1 (0.1-1.0 m from south) + W1 window (~2.5-4.1 m). East wall: bath/duct wall (0-2.7 m), 1.00 m passage opening (~2.7-3.7 m), kitchen wall (3.7-5.8 m). North: balcony D1 at the NE corner.
Positions read from the scan; confirm on site (about +/-0.15 m) before ordering joinery.

## Placement
- Entrance: shoe cabinet 0.90x0.35 on south wall beside the door arc (0.9 m radius kept clear).
- Living: 3-seater sofa 2.00x0.90 on west wall; TV unit 1.50x0.35 on east (bath) wall; ~2.5 m viewing distance; coffee table 1.00x0.50.
- Dining: table 1.60x0.85, 3 chairs on the south side + storage bench with 3 seats on the north wall = 6 seats. Optional crockery unit on west wall.
- Mandir (preferred): east (kitchen) wall of the dining, worshipper faces EAST, idol faces west. Not on the bath wall (toilet behind), clear of the balcony-door swing, ~0.75 m standing space in front.
- Alternative mandir: south wall (worshipper faces north) - conflicts with the shoe cabinet.
- Walkway: entrance -> passage ~0.9-1.1 m; dining -> balcony door clear.
